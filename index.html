Track 12 • Grammar

Used To

We use USED TO to talk about habits or facts in the past that are not true anymore.

THEN (Past)

"I used to play the guitar every day."

It was a habit.

NOW (Present)

"I play the piano now."

The past habit is finished. This is the new reality.

Track 13 • Structure

Forming Habits

After "used to", always use the base form of the verb.

➕ I used to sing.
➖ I didn't use to sing. *(Notice: no 'd' in use)*
❓ Did you use to sing?
